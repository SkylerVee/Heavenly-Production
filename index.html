Heavenly Production is a Kenyan film house built on one belief: the truest stories are the quiet ones: a mother's choice, a son's return, a stranger's kindness. We make films that follow the inner journey of our characters as closely as the outer one. Based in Nairobi, built for the world.

Why We Make Films

Heavenly Production was founded on a simple conviction: that emotional truth travels farther than special effects. We are a Nairobi-based media house that develops, produces, and distributes films centered on character: people caught between duty and desire, tradition and change, fear and hope. Our stories are Kenyan at the root, but universal in the questions they ask. We don't just follow what happens to our characters. We follow who they become.

Our Mission

To create films that move people: not just entertain them. To give Kenyan stories a global stage, told with craft, patience, and emotional honesty.

Our Values

1. Character First

Every story begins with a person, not a plot twist.

2. Emotional Depth

We take our time with feeling. No shortcuts to the heart.

3. Authentic Kenya

Our settings, language, and texture are ours, unapologetically.
